# V3.2 Slice 2.5 — Universe generation

**Branch:** `feature/v3-2-5-universe-generation` · **Merge target:** `develop/v3` only
**Migration:** no · **Infrastructure:** no · **Provider dependency:** none
**Feature flag:** `V3_UNIVERSE_PROVIDERS_ENABLED` (default **off**)

## Objective

Turn "which companies are in scope for this thesis?" from *one curated table* into a
composition of sources, de-duplicated by **listing identity** rather than by ticker
string, with every member recording which provider supplied it and whether its issuer
is actually identified.

## Why now

`market_universe_builder.THEME_COMPANY_REGISTRY` is a good bootstrap and it is the
whole universe. Two things follow: the platform can only research companies somebody
typed into a table, and de-duplication is by ticker string — which is the failure the
entity master exists to remove. This is the first slice that can key a universe on
identity, because 2.1 gave listings a venue key and 2.3 gave resolution a state.

## Proposed changes

### De-duplication by `(venue_key, ticker)`

`BA`@`XLON` and `BA`@`XNYS` are two members by construction. Two providers naming one
venue differently — `NASDAQ` and `US` — produce **one** member, because the venue key
comes from `exchange_registry` rather than from whichever string the provider used.

### Two candidates merge only on evidence

They become one member when they share a listing identity, **or** when both resolve to
the same `LegalEntity` on actionable evidence. If either resolution is `ambiguous`,
`conflicting` or `unresolved`, they stay separate. A duplicate member costs one wasted
research slot; a wrong merge researches one company under another's name.

An unresolved candidate is **not excluded** — a company the platform has never seen is
exactly what a universe is for. It enters with `resolution_state` recorded and
`legal_entity_id` NULL, and `is_identified` is the single check a caller makes before
treating a member as a known issuer.

### The merge check comes before the cap check

Found by the review pass. A candidate that merges into an already-accepted member adds
no member, so refusing it for "cap reached" throws away information the universe could
absorb for free *and* reports an exclusion for a company that is in fact present.

### `HARD_MAX_UNIVERSE_SIZE` (50) is enforced before anything expensive

Including before identity resolution, which is the only query in the loop. A full
company research run measures **261-451 seconds** and five concurrent analyses already
exceed the stale threshold on the deployed B1 plan, so an uncontrolled universe is not
a slow feature — it is an outage. Truncation is reported (`truncated_by_cap`), never
silent. Nothing in the module fetches, enriches or analyses, and a test asserts that
by scanning for identifiers in executable position.

### The curated registry is demoted, not deleted

`CuratedRegistryUniverseProvider` **delegates to `build_universe`** rather than reaching
into the registry, so the region and exclusion filters, the recorded exclusion reasons,
the pre-scan relevance score and the refusal on a vague thesis are the *same code* that
runs today. A second implementation of a filter is a second set of rules to keep in
agreement. It stays because it is the only source with a hand-verified reason for every
entry, and V3's rule is that nothing is deleted before a validated replacement exists.

### The entity master as a second universe

Every company the platform has researched has a `SecurityListing`.
`EntityMasterUniverseProvider` turns that into candidates — a universe with real
identity that improves as the platform is used. It is deliberately second: it carries
**no thesis relevance** and says so in its `relevance_reason`, because claiming one it
did not compute would let a downstream ranking read "we have seen this company" as
"this company fits the thesis". Its query is bounded by the cap **in the SQL**; loading
the table and slicing afterwards has already paid for the scan the cap prevents.

## Security considerations

No network, no migration. The composer contains every provider failure: one source
raising records an error against its own contribution and the others still run.
`resolver` and `session` are injected, so a caller with no database still gets a
universe and this module has no opinion about when identity is consulted.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **One ticker on two venues is two members; one company from two providers is one
   member** — and an `ambiguous` resolution never merges two candidates.
2. **The cap bounds the universe before identity is resolved**, truncation is reported,
   and a merge is not refused by a full cap.
3. **The V2 path is untouched**: with the flag off no provider is consulted, and
   `build_universe` still answers on its own exactly as it does on `main`.

## What the review pass found

1. **The cap was checked before the merge**, so a full universe dropped free
   information and reported a spurious exclusion. Reordered, with a test.
2. **`target.metadata_not_sourced = target.metadata_not_sourced and False`** — always
   `False`, which was the intent but written so obscurely that the next reader would
   have had to evaluate it to find out. Simplified, and both directions pinned by a
   test: the flag may be cleared by a richer provider and is never set by a thinner
   one, because a provider knowing less is not evidence that metadata is unavailable.
3. A third finding was the **same prose-versus-code trap for the third time** — a
   substring scan for `enrich` fired on the docstring explaining that nothing here
   enriches. Now uses `tests/helpers/source_scan.py`, which exists precisely because
   this keeps recurring.

## Non-goals

An external symbol-search provider (EODHD/SEC full index), which is a network path and
belongs with the provider runtime in V3.4 · semantic/theme ranking, which
`discovery_thesis_scoring` already does downstream · wiring the composer into the
discovery endpoint, which is a behaviour change to a live path and needs the flag
validated first · removing the curated registry.

## Rollback

`git revert` the merge. No migration, and the flag is off by default.
