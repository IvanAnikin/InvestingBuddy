### Run 2 — job `036d90a2-5755-4d29-af79-8199f1719a1d`, report `347512a1-4c02-4218-a5bb-1dfa0fc71caa`, API `d5cb416` (2026-09-22)

`OBSERVED IN PRODUCTION`: 54 findings, `is_complete_analysis: true`, 339 chunks indexed
before planning, 3 rounds / 12 tasks / 92 tool calls / 15 external searches, 8
restatements referenced rather than repeated.

Read critically, it still failed four ways:

1. The corpus held the 2026-Q2 10-Q and the 10-K's **exhibit index**, not the 10-K — so
   the business, segment, reserves and production questions had nothing to read, and
   several findings were exhibit-list trivia.
2. Profitability, revenue trajectory and valuation were re-asked from run 1's gaps
   **without** the statements tool: the prior-gap restoration copied the definition field
   by field and missed `optional_tools`.
3. Statements about what the evidence lacked ("No cited evidence names AI hardware…")
   were recorded as **findings**; they graded thesis dimensions `evidenced` and led the
   executive synthesis. One inferred "exposure reads as weak" from absence — the D3
   defect in a new place.
4. All 16 editor sentences were rejected `cites_no_finding`: the model grouped its labels
   as `[F1, F2]`.

Fixes (PR #225): the latest annual and quarterly reports are secured into the corpus
through the V3.16 bridge **before** planning; the prior-gap restoration takes the whole
definition; statements about the evidence become platform gaps (and an absence-derived
"weak" conclusion with them), with the writers told so and the thesis question saying
absence in what was read is not a weak link; grouped citations are citations; the
deterministic synthesis leads with each section's most informative finding.

### Run 4 — job `b33f10ce-8e68-42f1-bd5a-c88b683d6ebb`, report
`76dd57eb-2ad9-49d2-adf5-9619d00e4dd6`, API `b714404` (2026-09-23)

`OBSERVED IN PRODUCTION`: the first run that reads as research about a company.
Core filings ready (10-K `0001104659-26-021492`, 939 chunks indexed), council convened,
49 findings, `is_complete_analysis: true`, 3 rounds / 10 tasks / 64 tool calls, 11
restatements referenced rather than repeated, 5 of 30 external searches spent, no
degraded entries, editor kept 5 synthesis sentences and 9 leads.

**The FY2025 figures are correct**, checked line by line against the 10-K: net sales
13,420.0; operating income 7,001.7; net income 4,348.2; operating cash flow 4,752.1;
free cash flow 3,426.8 (= OCF − capex 1,325.3); diluted EPS 5.24; total assets 21,381.4;
equity 11,038.1; cash 4,304.6; long-term debt 6,750.7 — all USD millions, all group,
all FY2025. **No gross-profit line was invented** (the baseline's 2,914.8 was FY2019),
and cash conversion 109.3% is read in the correct direction. The business content is the
filing's own: 80–90% of metal production sold under annual or longer-term contracts, the
La Caridad complex, cathode purities, S-K 1300 reserves, the Arequipa litigation.

Read critically, it still failed four ways — and the causes were measured against the
production database rather than guessed:

1. **40 gaps said the filing's own figures "do not appear"** while the corpus held them.
   Every corpus query was the analyst question in prose; the index has no stopword list,
   so function words decided the ranking. The ledger shows every one of the 33 corpus
   calls returning exactly 6 items for a query like *"Which commodities and materials
   does the company produce and sell…"*. Asked instead for `net sales product revenue
   share`, the same corpus returns *"Net sales in 2025 reached a record high of
   $13,420.0 million"*, the 2,108.2-million-pound copper production, the operating cash
   cost per pound, the $1.10 quarterly dividend and the COFICAB rod contract.
2. **Thesis dimensions were graded `evidenced` on findings that never named them** — a
   finding about the exhibit index graded "semiconductors", and "What would change the
   thesis" then listed **no** unestablished dimension.
3. **Every finding built on the subject's own SEC statements carried no period.**
4. **A spacer grid took the top rank for three of seven questions** — `colspan` repeats
   and zero-width cells stuffing the term frequency.

Fixes (PR #227, V3.18.10): a corpus query is the question's own search intent reduced to
distinctive terms, `top_k` 8, the corpus rung continuing from the next intent; a thesis
dimension graded only on findings that name it; a tool's `period` read as the evidence's
period; table rows rendered without spacer cells or consecutive repeats.
