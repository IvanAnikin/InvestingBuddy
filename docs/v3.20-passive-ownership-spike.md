# V3.20 slice 3.1 — passive / ETF ownership: source spike

Checked 2026-11-15 against sec.gov and data.sec.gov with the research User-Agent. Only public pages, the two data-set readmes (PDF) and two fund registrants' EDGAR submission indexes were read. **No data-set ZIP was downloaded** (each N-PORT quarter is 390–690 MB, so a download needs the owner's go-ahead; see "What is still open").

## Form N-PORT data sets (holdings)

- **Where:** `https://www.sec.gov/files/dera/data/form-n-port-data-sets/<yyyy>q<n>_nport.zip`, quarterly from 2019Q4 to 2026Q3. Readme: `https://www.sec.gov/files/nport_readme.pdf`.
- **Size (2026Q3):** 440.95 MB. The range is 357–691 MB per quarter (2022Q3 is the largest).
- **The quarter is a FILING quarter.** "Data contained in documents filed after 5:30 PM Eastern on the last business day of a quarter will be included in the subsequent quarterly posting." A data set therefore holds the NPORT-P / NPORT-P/A filings disseminated in that quarter, whatever their report dates.
- **Publication lag, measured on EDGAR** (`data.sec.gov/submissions`):
  - iShares Trust: 1,449 NPORT-P filings, 1,448 of them filed 50–59 days after their `reportDate`.
  - SPDR Series Trust: 598 of 599 filed 50–59 days after.
  - Each series files the third month of its own fiscal quarter, so across series every calendar month appears.
  - **Consequence:** a quarter's data set carries holdings dated about 2–5 months before the posting. The newest iShares report month on 2026-11-15 was 2026-08 (filed 2026-10-26).
- **Tables used** (readme §5), all TSV:
  - `SUBMISSION` (`ACCESSION_NUMBER`, `FILING_DATE`, `SUB_TYPE`, `REPORT_DATE`);
  - `REGISTRANT` (`CIK`, `REGISTRANT_NAME`, `LEI`);
  - `FUND_REPORTED_INFO` (`SERIES_NAME`, `SERIES_ID`, `SERIES_LEI`, `NET_ASSETS`);
  - `FUND_REPORTED_HOLDING` (`HOLDING_ID`, `ISSUER_NAME`, `ISSUER_LEI`, `ISSUER_CUSIP`, `BALANCE`, `UNIT`, `CURRENCY_VALUE`, `PERCENTAGE` of the fund's net assets, `ASSET_CAT`, `PAYOFF_PROFILE`, `INVESTMENT_COUNTRY`);
  - `IDENTIFIERS` (`IDENTIFIER_ISIN`, `IDENTIFIER_TICKER`).
- **Amendments:** NPORT-P/A rows are in the data set beside the originals. The ingest keeps the newest filing per (series, report date).

## Form N-CEN data sets (is it an ETF?)

- **Where:** `https://www.sec.gov/files/dera/data/form-n-cen-data-sets/<yyyy>q<n>_ncen.zip`, 5.6 MB for 2026Q3. Readme: `https://www.sec.gov/files/ncen_readme.pdf`.
- **The ETF flag exists:** `FUND_REPORTED_INFO.IS_ETF` is 'Y' when fundType = 'Exchange-Traded Fund' (Item C.3.a.i). Also present: `IS_ETMF`, `IS_INDEX` (index fund, C.3.b) and `IS_INDEX_AFFILIATED` / `IS_INDEX_EXCLUSIVE`.
  - Rows are keyed by `FUND_ID` = accession + CIK + series ID, so the series ID joins N-PORT's `FUND_REPORTED_INFO.SERIES_ID`.
  - Section 5.44 `ETF` and 5.42 `SECURITY_EXCHANGE` add creation-unit data and the listing exchange and ticker.
- **Known gap (stated by the SEC on the data-set page):** "The data sets do not include, at this time, N-CEN filings submitted in the schema version 3.1 introduced in EDGAR version 25.2."
  - N-CEN is annual, so a fund whose newest N-CEN was filed in schema 3.1 falls back to its previous filing, or is unknown.
  - **So `is_etf` is three-valued:** true, false, or null (not known). A null is never shown as "not an ETF".

## Decisions taken for 3.1

- `funds` keys on the EDGAR series ID. `is_etf` and `is_index` are nullable, with the source (`ncen`) and the N-CEN filing date it came from.
- `security_passive_ownership` holds one row per (identifier kind, identifier, data-set period, source kind):
  - counts, shares and value, plus the report-date range of the holdings in it;
  - the top 20 funds as JSON;
  - the source URL.

  Raw holdings are not kept (spec §3.3).
- `fund_holdings_ingests` makes an ingest idempotent per (source kind, period) and records what it wrote, or why it failed.

## First real run (2026-11-16, local scratch database)

The 2026Q3 N-PORT ZIP (462,371,568 bytes, 55 s to download) and the N-CEN ZIPs for 2025Q4–2026Q3 (5.9–16.4 MB each) were read for four targets. The owner had delegated the go-ahead.

| | POWL | UCTT | BW | AAPL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Funds holding | 393 | 338 | 129 | 2,126 |
| of which ETFs (N-CEN) / ETF status unknown | 128 / 25 | 86 / 30 | 18 / 6 | 555 / 173 |
| Shares held (`UNIT = NS`) | 13,945,385 | 25,650,851 | 35,896,175 | 3,540,215,859 |
| Non-share lines (kept apart) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 |
| Report dates | 2025-09-30 … 2026-07-31 | 2025-09-30 … 2026-07-31 | 2026-05-31 … 2026-07-31 | 2026-05-29 … 2026-07-31 |

- **Scale:** the members `FUND_REPORTED_HOLDING.tsv` (952 MB, 5,627,083 rows) and `IDENTIFIERS.tsv` (269 MB) dominate. There were 13,300 filings kept and 29 superseded by a later filing for the same series and report date. 3,043 holdings matched a target.
- **Speed:**
  - The first run took **3 hours**; peak memory was 107 MB. The cause: a `re.sub` plus a regex match on every row, and a `dict` per CSV row.
  - Slice 3.1b reads only the needed columns as tuples and looks up a whitespace-free upper-case key. The full regex runs only on a hit. With that, aggregation takes **333 s at 60 MB** on the developer laptop, and the counts are identical.
- **Plausibility:** POWL's 13.95M shares are about 38% of its 36.43M cover-page shares (2026-08-03), and AAPL's are about 24%. Both are in the expected range for US-registered funds.
- **Stale reports:** a series that filed an older report in this filing quarter (late or amended) brings report dates back to 2025-09-30. The newest report per series wins, so these are series with no newer report in the data set. The report's freshness note covers this.

## What is still open

- **Whether `BALANCE` with `UNIT = 'NS'` covers every equity holding of interest.** Depositary receipts and other units are kept apart and not added up. In the real file, AAPL had 2 non-share lines and the three small caps had none.
- **Production run:** the ingest has run only locally. Running it against the live database needs the V3 deployment approval (CLAUDE.md).
