## 1. What V3.16.1a measured, and what V3.16.1b changed

V3.16.1a shipped diagnostics only. Its production run
(`c2e8084e-bf1f-4713-a455-1a7fa5db75d6`, 2026-09-17, SHA `b8e5ab3`) recorded:

```json
{"responses_total": 8, "responses_truncated": 8, "responses_unparseable": 8,
 "responses_with_findings": 0, "finish_reasons": {"length": 8}}
```

Eight investigator write-ups, eight replies cut off at the output ceiling, **zero
findings**. Case A, measured off the provider's own `finish_reason`.

The measured cause was **over-production, not an under-sized budget**: `_write_up` keeps
`[:MAX_FINDINGS_PER_QUESTION]` findings and the same number of gaps, and the prompt never
said so. V3.16.1b tells the model those caps (derived from the constant), adds per-field
bounds and a token target, forwards `json_mode`, raises the ceiling 1,200 → 1,800 off a
measured distribution, and retries once — tighter — when a reply truncates. Three further
defects found in review are recorded in §7 of
[`v3.16.1b-root-cause-measurement.md`](v3.16.1b-root-cause-measurement.md).

## 8. HYPOTHESES — stated as hypotheses

### 8.1 The chair's "0 findings" is the council pack's state, not the Ledger's

The deterministic chair reported:

> "Deterministic synthesis: 0 finding(s), 0 verified, 33 open gap(s) … this is the
> ledger's own state rather than an interpretation of it."

while the Ledger holds 18. Reading `council_v2/inputs.py`: the blocking-gap refusal branch
returns a `CouncilInput` **without a `findings=` argument**, so it defaults to empty, and
the chair prints `len(council_input.findings)`. The count is therefore correct *for the
council pack* and the sentence "this is the ledger's own state" is **wrong in the refusal
case**.

*Hypothesis, not a measured defect:* this is a wording bug, not data loss — the findings
are in the Ledger and `consumption.findings_total` reports them. **Not changed here.**
Altering chair semantics is outside V3.16.1b and would be exactly the "make the Council
green" move the brief forbids. It is worth a small slice of its own.

### 8.2 Production replies run longer than the bench predicted

The bench measured shaped replies at median 845 / max 1,763 tokens. Production averaged
**1,267.8** output tokens per investigator call — above the bench median, on real evidence.

*Hypothesis:* the probe's evidence block was less rich than a live run's, so the bench
under-stated the true distribution. Two consequences follow, and both are honest:

- it **corroborates** the original defect — a mean of ~1,268 against a 1,200 ceiling is
  exactly a population that truncates nearly always, which is what 8/8 was;
- the 1,800 ceiling carries **less margin than the bench suggested**. It held 6/6 here
  with zero truncation, and the retry exists precisely for the day it does not.
