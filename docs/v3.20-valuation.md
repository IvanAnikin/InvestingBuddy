# V3.20 Phase 8 — Valuation and financial depth

Spec: [§3.8–3.9](v3.20-electricity-vertical-spec.md). Plan: [Phase 8](v3.20-implementation-plan.md).
This page describes what is built. It grows as each slice lands.

## 8.1 Enterprise value and multiples (`services/valuation/enterprise_value.py`, `pipeline/enterprise_value_step.py`)

Deterministic arithmetic over dated, cited inputs; no model call, no estimate, no currency
conversion.

| Figure | Inputs | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Market cap | The run's analysis-time observation (slice 1.3), native currency | With no thesis observation, an exchange-directory re-read only with `V3_ENTERPRISE_VALUE_RECHECK_ENABLED` (off). A float-floor figure is flagged `lower_bound`, and so is the EV built on it. |
| Net debt | `total_debt − cash_and_equivalents`, group-scope `ExtractedFact` rows (inline XBRL first) | The newest **annual** period holding both in one currency; both facts cited. Negative = net cash, a real answer. |
| EV | market cap + net debt | Refused on `currency_mismatch`, `stale_balance_sheet` (filed more than 400 days before the market-cap date), `undated`, `no_market_cap`, `no_net_debt`. |
| EV / EBIT | EV ÷ newest annual `operating_profit` | `not_meaningful` when EBIT ≤ 0 — never a negative multiple. |
| EV per capacity unit | EV ÷ each disclosed capacity reading (slice 5.4) | One multiple per unit; never summed across units. |
| EV / backlog | — | `not_available`: no disclosed order backlog is held as a fact yet. |
| Own-history range | — | `not_available`: needs a dated price series; only today's market cap is held. |

**Report.** `sections[valuation_context].enterprise_value`, present only when the block
had a market cap or a balance sheet (other reports are byte-identical). Web:
`EnterpriseValueSection`. Flag `V3_ENTERPRISE_VALUE_ENABLED` (default on, no external call).

**Not in this slice.** Interim (TTM) EBIT, minority interests / preferred equity / leases
in EV, a price-history store for own-history ranges, a backlog fact.

## 8.2 Peers by layer (`services/valuation/layer_peers.py`)

The subject's electricity layer (slice 5.2) selects the newest research record of every
other company in the same layer from the comparable-set store (slice 9.1,
`research_records.comparable_set`). Each peer shows its own record's market cap (USD, dated),
EV/EBIT and currency, obscurity score, and its size relative to the subject.

ADR-089 still applies on top: same layer is the function test, size the second. A peer
whose market cap is outside 0.2×–5× the subject's is listed with `comparable_size: false`
and left out of the median. `median_ev_to_ebit` is given only over at least 3
size-comparable peers in ONE currency; otherwise it is `null` with `median_reason`.

**Report.** `sections[valuation_context].layer_peers` — only for a classified company with at
least one other record in its layer. Web: `LayerPeersSection`. Flag
`V3_LAYER_PEERS_ENABLED` (default on, no external call).

**Not in this slice.** Peers the platform has not researched (the store only compounds as
runs accumulate); the ADR-089 tool path (`get_peer_set`) still selects by canonical
industry and is unchanged.

## 8.4 What today's price implies (`services/valuation/market_implied.py`, `pipeline/market_implied_step.py`)

Reverse arithmetic over the dated EV (8.1), the layer-peer median (8.2) and the issuer's
own filed figures → `sections[valuation_context].market_implied`, only when an EV was
computed (`V3_MARKET_IMPLIED_ENABLED`, default on, no external call).

| Part | Formula / rule | Fails closed when |
|---|---|---|
| `peer_position` | subject EV/EBIT ÷ peer median: `below_peer_median` ≤ 0.85×, `above_peer_median` ≥ 1.15×, else `in_line` | no computed multiple or no median (8.2 needs ≥ 3 size-comparable peers) |
| `implied_margin` | `EV ÷ peer median EV/EBIT ÷ revenue` — the operating margin at which today's EV equals the peer median multiple; shown beside the filed margin | no median; no annual revenue in the EV's currency |
| `implied_growth` | `g = (r·EV − FCF) ÷ (EV + FCF)`, FCF = operating cash flow − capex (newest annual period holding both, EV's currency), for r on a **named illustrative grid** (8 %, 10 %, 12 %) labelled an assumption, not a cited cost of capital | FCF ≤ 0 → `not_meaningful`; no period with both figures → `not_available` |
| `discount_characteristics` | only when below the peer median: smaller than the median peer, net debt > 3× EBIT, ≥ 25 % of debt due within two years, diluted shares up > 5 %, obscurity ≥ 60, < 5 % fund-held, a disclosed parent, a non-USD price, ≥ 2 quarters below the held EPS expectation — each with its block, observed value and threshold | — (an empty list says none was observed) |

The characteristics are **observations that often accompany a lower multiple; none is
established as the cause**. Operating cash flow is after interest paid, so the FCF basis
is not a pure firm-level cash flow; the block says so. The words describe what the market
price implies — no value judgement is made.

**Not in this slice.** A cited discount rate (needs the 8.6 inputs); an implied-margin
path over time; the reason classification as a model judgement.

## 8.5 Financial depth (`sources/sec_financial_depth.py`, `pipeline/financial_depth_step.py`) — partial

For a company with an SEC CIK, nine `companyconcept` reads (research User-Agent, SEC rate
limiter); an untagged concept (HTTP 404) is `not_disclosed`, never zero. Annual filings
only (10-K, 10-K/A, 10-KT).

| Item | Concepts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Debt maturity ladder | `LongTermDebtMaturitiesRepaymentsOfPrincipal{InNextTwelveMonths, InYearTwo…InYearFive, AfterYearFive}` | ONE balance-sheet date (the newest year-end tagged), each bucket from the newest annual filing for that date; total only when all six are tagged in one currency. |
| Dilution | `WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding` (duration ≈ 1 year) | Per fiscal year, newest filing's value, year-on-year change; `latest_filing_restated` when a later filing changed a year's count (a split) — not adjusted. |
| Contract balances | `ContractWithCustomerAssetNet`, `ContractWithCustomerLiability` | Year-end series: unbilled revenue, and customer advances / deferred revenue — quality-of-earnings inputs, not a verdict. |

**Report.** `sections[financial_capacity].financial_depth`, only when at least one concept is
tagged. Web: `FinancialDepthSection`. Flag `V3_FINANCIAL_DEPTH_ENABLED` (default on).

**Not in this slice.** Capitalised development, POC revenue and working-capital /
prepayment trends; a margin bridge; IFRS (20-F) filers; non-US filers.

## 8.6 Valuation engine (`services/calculations/valuation_engine.py`) — partial, engine only

ADR-099: a deterministic engine computes; a model may only propose assumptions. Inputs are
`Cited` (value, source URL, period, currency) or `Assumption` (value, rationale, who
proposed it); every one is listed in the output.

- **Methods.** `fcff_dcf` — five explicit years from a cited base FCFF at the scenario's
  growth path, Gordon terminal value, WACC from components (CAPM cost of equity = risk-free
  + beta × ERP + size premium; after-tax cost of debt; debt weight ≤ 0.9); equity = EV −
  cited net debt; per share ÷ cited diluted shares. `peer_multiple` — the 8.2 layer-peer
  median EV/EBIT × cited EBIT, adjusted by the scenario's named discount/premium.
- **Scenarios.** bear / base / bull, each a full set of named assumptions.
- **Range.** Low = min of bear, mid = mean of base, high = max of bull across computed
  methods; only with at least 2 methods. Sensitivity grid for the DCF base case:
  discount rate ±1 pp × terminal growth ±0.5 pp.
- **Fail-closed.** `insufficient_evidence` and no number when: no cited base period, unknown
  shares or net debt, uncited risk-free rate / ERP, discount rate not above terminal growth,
  or fewer than 2 methods.

**Wiring (`services/valuation/engine_inputs.py`, `pipeline/valuation_step.py`).** The engine
runs when `V3_VALUATION_ENGINE_ENABLED` is on (default **off**: an investment-research
opinion, private use only) and the run has a computed EV. Its output goes to the report's
top-level `professional_research.valuation`. It is attached after the editor, so no model
reads or restates it.

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| net debt, EBIT | the 8.1 EV block (cited filing) |
| diluted shares | newest year of the 8.5 dilution series (cited filing) |
| base FCFF | operating cash flow − capital expenditure, newest annual period holding both (8.4 `pick_annual`) |
| layer-peer median EV/EBIT | the 8.2 block (comparable-set records) |
| risk-free rate | FRED `DGS10`, newest published day (`fredgraph.csv`, no key; USD only; `V3_VALUATION_RISK_FREE_FETCH_ENABLED`) |
| equity risk premium | `V3_EQUITY_RISK_PREMIUM_JSON` = `{"value", "source_url", "as_of"}`, entered by the owner **with** its publication. Empty (the default) → the DCF is `insufficient_evidence`; no ERP is ever made up |
| growth | named: compound growth of the held annual revenues (≤ 4 years), bounded −5%..15%; bear −3 pp, bull +3 pp; zero if fewer than two years |
| terminal growth, beta, multiple adjustment | named: 1.5/2/2.5%; 1.2/1.0/0.9 (no cited beta held); −15%/0/+10% |
| size premium, cost of debt, tax, debt weight | named: 2% under USD 2bn; risk-free + 2 pp; 21% (USD) / 25%; net debt ÷ (net debt + market cap), 0..0.6 |

All named assumptions carry `proposed_by: "engine_default"`.

**The price.** The price is the dated market cap ÷ diluted shares, with both dates shown
(`price_reference`). `relative_position` (`undervalued | in_line | overvalued`: the price
below, within or above the range) is the only ADR-099 term. The gate allows it at that path
only. `upside_downside_pct` gives the range ends and mid against the price.

**Consumers:**
- the 9.2 verdict, where the valuation pillar moves ±2 and a computed range lifts the cap;
- the record, as `record_json.valuation` (the range, price, position and upside/downside only);
- the portfolio review, where a price that moves up through the range since entry is a
  weakening item;
- the web, through `ValuationRangeSection`.

**Not built.** A model-proposed assumption set; an ECB risk-free rate for non-USD issuers
(they fail closed on the DCF); 8.7 inputs (contracted cash flows, RAB, segments, replacement
capacity) from filings, so those methods stay absent; precedent transactions (8.3).

## 8.7 Further engine methods (`services/calculations/valuation_engine.py`) — partial, engine only

Each method runs only when its inputs are given; without them it is absent from the output
(the 8.6 output is unchanged) and never counted towards the range.

| Method | Inputs | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `contracted_dcf` | cited contracted cash flows, one per future year (offtake / PPA / availability payments); cited risk-free rate; scenario `contract_spread` | Σ CF_t ÷ (1 + rf + spread)^t — **no terminal value**: a contract ends |
| `rab_multiple` | cited regulated asset base; scenario `rab_multiple` | RAB × multiple |
| `sotp` | ≥ 2 `Segment`s (cited metric, named multiple); scenario `multiple_adjustment` | Σ metric × multiple × (1 + adjustment), parts listed |
| `multiple:<key>` | `PeerMultiple` (cited subject metric, cited peer median, e.g. EV per MW) | median × (1 + adjustment) × metric |
| replacement-cost **floor** | `Replacement` (cited capacity, unit, named cost per unit); scenario `replacement_haircut` (0 ≤ h < 1) | capacity × cost × (1 − h), under `floors` — a floor, **not** a method, so it never moves the range |

Every new input is listed under `inputs` (cited figures, contracted cash flows, segments,
peer multiples) or `inputs.assumptions` (per-scenario spread, RAB multiple, haircut; the
cost per unit). Per share uses the same cited net debt and diluted shares as 8.6, and
fails closed without them.

**Not in this slice.** Wiring (as 8.6: needs 0.3 in the stack); sources for contracted cash
flows, RAB and replacement cost per unit; the rendering of the range against the dated
price (needs the 0.3 allow-list for its vocabulary).
