## 12.2 Review (`services/portfolio_review.py`, `GET /api/v1/portfolios/{id}/review`)

Deterministic and versioned (`RULES_VERSION`); no model call, nothing written, nothing sent
outside the process. Per holding:

1. **Records.** The research record at or before the entry date (else the first one after
   it, labelled so) and the newest record (slice 9.1).
2. **Changes since entry.** The 9.4 record diff between those two records, plus every
   monitoring signal for the company observed since the entry date (deduplicated by key),
   plus EV/EBIT drift between the records.
3. **Weakening items** (counted): a customer or parent no longer held; a consensus revision
   with a lower EPS average; fund ownership down ≥ 0.5 pp; ≥ 2 quarters below the held
   EPS expectation in the newest record; a same-layer competitor's capacity rise; EV/EBIT
   ≥ 25 % lower than at the entry record. Everything else that changed is **context**,
   listed and not counted. Each item carries its source URL where the evidence has one.
4. **Thesis status.**

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `broken` | ≥ 3 weakening items of ≥ 2 kinds |
| `weakened` | ≥ 1 weakening item |
| `intact` | no weakening item, and researched again (or observed) since entry |
| `insufficient_evidence` | not linked to a researched company; no record; or nothing researched or observed since entry |

5. **Proposed research** — research changes only (ADR-100 §3): re-run the analysis (newest
   record older than 90 days, or weakened/broken), investigate each weakening kind, or
   research a company the platform does not hold.

**Verdicts (slice 9.2).** `entry_verdict` and `current_verdict` are read from each record's
`rubric_json` (`{state: "available", recommendation, meaning, composite_score,
confidence_band, record_as_of}`). A record without one shows `not_available`. A lower verdict
now than at entry is a weakening item (`verdict_change`); a higher one is context. The
summary text never names a label.

**Gate.** The route runs `gate_review` with `PORTFOLIO_REVIEW_ALLOWANCES`, which admit the
label only at `holdings[*].{entry,current}_verdict.recommendation`. The portfolio's own name
is excluded from the scan, because it is the user's words shown back to them. Any other hit
withholds both verdicts and records `safety_hits`. Tests also check sizing phrases: no
"increase the position", "reduce exposure", "rebalance", target weight or size.

**Not in this slice.** Valuation-range drift (needs the engine wired, 8.6);
an LLM narrative over the review (it would need the private-data policy, Open Decision #11).
