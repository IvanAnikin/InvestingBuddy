# V3.20 Phase 9 — Output, rubric, monitoring

Spec: [§3.10–3.11](v3.20-electricity-vertical-spec.md). Plan: [Phase 9](v3.20-implementation-plan.md).
This page describes what is built. It grows as each slice lands.

## 9.1 Comparable-set store (`services/research_records.py`, migration 053)

One structured record per completed research run, written after the report is assembled
(`V3_RESEARCH_RECORDS_ENABLED`). It is read from the report's own blocks —
`business_model.electricity_layers`, `operations.real_asset`,
`business_model.relationships`, `valuation_context.{enterprise_value, obscurity, passive_ownership}`,
`growth_and_catalysts.change_events`, `financial_capacity.earnings_track_record` — plus the
run's size check, so the three renderings (memo, report, record) come from one source.
Columns and rules: [DATABASE.md, migration 053](DATABASE.md).

`comparable_set(session, layer, exclude_company_id=None, limit=25)` — the newest record per
company in a layer, newest first: the peer source for slice 8.2.

**Not in this slice.** The rubric (`rubric_json`, slice 9.2); a reader in the web app; peer
selection from the set (8.2).

## 9.2 Rubric and computed verdict (`services/research_verdict.py`, ADR-098)

`verdict_block(report, valuation=None)` scores eight pillars from the assembled report and
computes `PASS | WATCHLIST | SHORTLIST`. It is deterministic and calls no model. It runs
after the editor, so no model sees or chooses the label. Flag:
`V3_RESEARCH_VERDICT_ENABLED` (default **off**: an investment-research opinion, private use
only). Output: `professional_research.verdict`, also stored as the record's `rubric_json`.

| Pillar | Weight | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| Asset quality | 15% | findings in `operations` |
| Financial quality and balance sheet (key) | 15% | findings in `financial_capacity`; leverage, refinancing wall and dilution from `market_implied.discount_characteristics` (−1 each); net cash from `enterprise_value` (+1) |
| Valuation (key) | 15% | findings in `valuation_context`; the ADR-099 position against the range (±2) when a `valuation` block exists, otherwise the peer position from `market_implied` (±1) |
| Competitive position (key) | 15% | findings in `competitive_position` |
| Structural tailwind | 10% | findings in `industry_and_market`; share of thesis dimensions evidenced (±1) |
| Catalysts | 10% | findings in `growth_and_catalysts`; change events (+1); an EPS streak of ≥ 2 quarters (±1) |
| Management and governance | 10% | governance-domain findings in `risks_and_counter_thesis` and `business_model` |
| Under-researched edge | 10% | the measured obscurity score (80+ → 5, 60+ → 4, 40+ → 3, 20+ → 2, else 1) |

**Scoring.** A pillar starts at 3. The supportive/adverse balance of its findings moves it
by at most 2 (one finding moves it by 1). Each block signal moves it by 1. The result is
clamped to 1–5. A pillar with no directional finding and no signal is **not scored**: it
is not a silent 3. Each score lists its finding labels, with source quality, and its
signals. A finding cited only from open-web sources is *thin*.

**Confidence** = covered weight × (0.5 + 0.5 × share of primary evidence). Bands: high
≥ 0.7, medium ≥ 0.45.

**Verdict** (thresholds in `THRESHOLDS`, versioned `RUBRIC_VERSION`):
- The composite is the weight-renormalised mean of the scored pillars.
- SHORTLIST needs a composite ≥ 3.6 and no cap.
- WATCHLIST needs a composite ≥ 3.0.
- Otherwise the verdict is PASS.

Caps, each of which forbids SHORTLIST:
- a key pillar unscored or more than half thin;
- no computed valuation range (ADR-099 fails closed);
- any pillar scored 1;
- confidence < 0.6;
- covered weight < 0.5.

**Gate.** The label is allowed only at `verdict.recommendation`
(`safety_terms.VERDICT_ALLOWANCE`). `v3_pipeline._report_safety_hits` scans the `verdict`
and `valuation` roots with `V320_REPORT_ALLOWANCES`, and everything else without
allowances. No other string in the block names a label. The ADR-099 position is written
there as a neutral code (`price_below_range`, …). The web renders the label beside its
meaning and a disclaimer (`ResearchVerdictSection`).

**Valuation input.** The verdict reads `professional_research.valuation` (slice 8.6 wiring).
Without a computed range, which is the case when the engine is off or the ERP is not
entered, every verdict is capped below SHORTLIST. That is the fail-closed behaviour ADR-099
asks for.

**Not in this slice.** The legacy `real_asset_report_completer` (the V2 admin draft
completed to `report_schema.json`) still emits its stand-in `PASS` with every pillar at 3.
Its draft is not the V3 report, and its gate has no allowance for `report_meta.conviction`.
An LLM-written rationale is also not built: the rationale is deterministic text.

## 9.3 Renderings and the mandatory self-critique (`pipeline/self_critique.py`)

**Self-critique.** `professional_research.self_critique` is assembled after the verdict
from what the run recorded. It is deterministic and calls no model.
`V3_SELF_CRITIQUE_ENABLED` is on by default, because the block is additive and gives no
opinion. The block holds:

- **Bear case:**
  - counter-thesis findings and every adverse finding, cited **by label** (a finding is
    stated once, in its own section);
  - each Red Team challenge the responder could not resolve (`challenges.unresolved_items`),
    in the Red Team model's words, clipped to 400 characters. A challenge whose text trips
    the safety gate is withheld and counted in `challenges_withheld_for_safety`.
- **Weakest links:** rubric pillars scored ≤ 2 or more than half open-web (when 9.2 ran),
  thesis dimensions not established, and blocking questions left open.
- **Data-quality warnings:** findings in financial capacity, competitive position,
  valuation context and operations that are cited only from open-web sources, plus the count
  of platform evidence gaps.
- **Disconfirming search:** counts of adverse, counter-thesis and supportive findings, and
  what the Red Team round did.

`state` is `complete` only when the Red Team raised at least one challenge **and** the bear
case holds an item. Otherwise it is `incomplete`, with a reason, and `publication_ready` is
false (spec §3.10).

**Three renderings of one stored report:**
1. The **web report** (`/research/reports/[id]`). It now carries the verdict, the
   valuation range and the self-critique panels.
2. The **print view**, `/research/reports/[id]/print`: the full report laid out for paper.
   Its button opens the browser's print dialog, and "Save as PDF" is the PDF export.
   `@media print` in `globals.css` hides navigation and the background and switches the
   tokens to dark ink on white.
3. The **one-page memo**, `…/print?format=memo` (`ResearchMemo`). It shows the verdict
   with its meaning and caps, the valuation range against the dated price, up to five
   synthesis sentences with their labels, the catalyst and counter-thesis labels, up to
   four bear-case items and both disclaimers.

The structured record (9.1) is the third output named in the spec. The memo re-words
nothing: every sentence and figure is the report's own.

**Not in this slice.**
- A server-side PDF: there is no PDF library in the stack, so the browser's print-to-PDF
  is the export.
- An LLM-written bear-case narrative.
- The legacy `report_schema.json` `self_critique` (V2 completer) is unchanged.

## 9.4 Monitoring signals from the records (`monitoring/research_signals.py`, migration 054)

Six signal kinds for the electricity vertical, all derived from what the platform already
holds — no vendor call, no network. Each compares the company's **newest research record
with the previous one** (9.1), except where noted:

| Kind | Raised when | Never raised when |
|---|---|---|
| `earnings_released` | a filed quarter newer than any the previous record held | the previous record held no earnings track record (a first read is not a flood) |
| `consensus_revision` | the dated expectation for the same quarter has a new as-of date and a different EPS or revenue average | the as-of date is unchanged; no expectations are held (no estimates vendor configured) |
| `passive_ownership_change` | the % of shares outstanding held by US-registered funds moved ≥ 0.5 pp | either record did not hold the N-PORT figure |
| `relationship_change` | a customer or a (direct/ultimate) parent is newly held or no longer held | either record did not research that group (unknown is not "lost") |
| `change_event` | an event or insider cluster the previous record did not hold, **or** an event classified from a held disclosure dated after the last run | the previous record had no change-events block |
| `competitor_capacity_announcement` | a same-layer peer researched after this company's newest record disclosed ≥ 5 % more capacity (same unit) than at its own previous record | the peer has one record; the unit differs |

Both `change_event` paths derive the same `signal_key`, so one event cannot be raised
twice. Summaries say what changed, never what it means (checked with the safety scan).

**Wiring.** `detect_for_company` (V3.9) calls it when `V3_MONITORING_ENABLED` (default
**off**) and `V3_MONITORING_RESEARCH_SIGNALS_ENABLED` (default on) are both set. Nothing
calls `detect_for_company` on a schedule or at run end — the test that pins this stays:
cadence is [Open Decision #15](v3/OPEN_DECISIONS.md#15-monitoring-cadence), user-owned. The
spec's proposal (daily disclosure check, weekly estimates, quarterly N-PORT) is recorded
there, not configured.

**Not in this slice.** A trigger (#15); a web reader of signals; `consensus_revision` from a
live estimates vendor; competitor capacity from peers that were never researched.
